# Treasures and Trolls Basic Adventure Module B1 - Graystone Keep

Graystone keep is an introductory adventure module meant mostly as an example of how to create your own adventures.  The most important thing about the module is story telling, and for that reason each adventure may add new monsters, treasure, even new spells, character classes or other game mechanics.  Basic treasures and Trols is meant as a set of guidelines for story telling and it was meant to be adapted to the needs of the story you want to tell, like i this adventure module

# Background

Graystone keep is a small outpost in the borderland between two kingdoms.  It is far from any large cities in the middle of a wilderness filled with monsters, and adventure.  Their only defense is a small garrison, local villagers and a town militia who much keep the villagers safe from constant attacks by monsters who live in the surrounding area.  Recently, those attacks have become more frequent and dangerous.   The villagers believe several groups of monsters have moved in nearby, but they lack the resources to find and eliminated them.  If they send their soldiers or militia outside the town, the villagers will be left too vulnerable to an attack.  So, they are hoping some brave adventurers will help them find the threat and restore peace to their small village.

# Factions

Because the keep is situated in the wilderness between several nearby kingdoms, the local population is influenced by many factions from nearby kingdoms.  

**Kingdom of Rilian**
The kingdom of Rilian is a kingdom to the north and home of the Arcaneum, a university of magical and scientific research.  The kingdom of Rilian embrace magic as a force for good.

- **House Aelas** - The current ruling house of Rilian, house Aelas value justice.  King Dugal IX believes the kingdom serves its people.  His objective is the safety and wellbeing of all hsi subjects.

- **House Vaylor** - The rivals of house Ebonreach, on the surface the serve the king, but secretly work to undermine him and seize control.  Vaylor has come under the sway of agents and spies of Lokor, and soem nobles are secretly members of the Cult of Lokor.  Lord Morden is the leader of house Vaylor.

**Kindom of Arnsland**
The kingdom of Arnsland is a kingdom to the south.  In Arnsland, all magic is outlawed as it opens a door to demons and evil forces.  The arnish army is lead by clerics and paladins.  The keep is part of the kingdom of Arnsland, and the garrison is manned by Arnish soldiers, but being on the borderland, they do not enforce the ban on magic consistently, its more of a dont ask dont tell situation.

- **House Pendax** Lead by king Baylor, Pendax has been weakened by their rival house Acklund.  The other noble houses support king  only when compelled to do so, and it is increasingly difficult for the king to both maintain the peace and protect the commoners.  Although the king does fight for the well being of all his subjects, this is made difficult by nearly open rebellion form the nobles

- **House Acklund** Under the secret influence of spies of Lokor, Lord Lohn of house Acklund is trying to depose the king and take the throne for himself.  Although not yet in open rebellion, it is an open secret that they do not support the king.  Like Vaylor, many nobles in Acklund are secretly members of the cult of Lokor.

**Kingdom of Talerin**
The kingdom of Talerin is an island kingdom to the west made of several smaller islands.  They are a seafaring kingdom of fisherman.  Talerin is made up of many strongly independent clans that look out for eachother.  Their idea of justice is based on their clan and honor.  They are bound by their word and any alliances of their clans.  The confederacy itself is held together by a series of ancient alliances of the clans, which largely prevents open war amongst the clans, although blood feuds and interclan skirmishing is not uncommon.  These matters are often settled by forced consent from pressure from the other clans, through the high council of clans, who may judge who has been wronged and demand restitution be paid.

**Elvenwood**
King Eloras of Elvenwood consider the elves to be guardians of all Ur and the vanguard in the war against the Lokor and the Fomorians, their ancient enemy.  Their main objective in this war is to unite the other races, even the goblins, against their common enemy, and to put aside their differences and stand strong against the forces of the Fomorians.  Unfortunately they are seldom successful in uniting the other races.

**Northwood**
The elves of Northwood share the common enemy of the Fomorians, but have long ago given up trying to unite the other races, and view them with almost as much hostility as the Fomorians, mainly because they are constantly being deceived and manipulated by the Fomorians to work against eachother or in outright alliance with the Fomorians.  They are most likely to ally with other fey, even goblins, as they all share an ancient conflict with the Fomorians and are less likely to act as their puppets.

**Kalidor** A dwarven kingdom to the north, lead by their king Boralund, Kalidor is more welcome to form mutually beneficial trade alliances with other races.  They are still dwarves, their main interest is mining gold, gems, and other treasure.  They are extraordinarily wealthy and mistrust the elves.  

**Undor** Lead by king Thorundar, Undor is in many ways similar to Kalidor, but much more isolationist.  They mistrust other races nearly as much as the elves.  They form only limited trade alliances only to barter for what they need and are quite stingy to part with their precious treasure.  However, because of their great wealth, what trade the do have is often for fine ale and wine and other luxuries, sparing now expense for the finest fare.

**Order of Fire**
Seret order of spies from Dax, the kingdom of Dragons far to the north.  They work form the shadows and secretly influence political events in many kingdoms.  Their main objective is to prevent the Fomorians from becoming resurgent and becoming a threat to Dax.  As long as what happening way over there stays way over their, its fine.  They have access to enormous wealth and have gathered secret information on most human kingdoms, making them very powerful even among the human kingdoms.  Most are unaware of their presence.

**Thieves’ Guild**
A loose order of scofflaws, the thieves’ guild operate under the common principles that the idea of property ownership is determined by who is in charge.  They believe that kings and nobles are no better than thieves themselves, just thieves with armies and castles to enforce their idea of ownership.  They believe the only way to be truly free is to live by your own laws.

**Order or the gray hood**
A sub faction of the thieves’ guild, the gray hoods are both more radical and more egalitarian.  They reject the common law and in the spirit of robin hood, seek to redistribute the wealth of the feudal nobility to the common man.  They even go so far as to promote philanthropic charity.

**Travelers**
The travelers are nomadic wanderers, mainly from Talerin, that are part of a loose confederacy of clans which span the entire world of Ur.  There nomadic culture keeps traveler clans in contact with other travelers both near and far, which is how they form a common culture that spans many kingdoms and realms of Ur.  Local customs may vary from region to region, but they all share core values that place the honor of the clan above all else.  Travelers have no obligation to honor any agreement outside of other Travelers.  they can and do lie cheat and steal to make a living, simply because they don’t recognize any authority at all outside of the Travelers.  This is why they have a widespread reputation as liars and thieves.  It is possible to become a Friend of the Travelers through and act that shows exceptional honor, bravery, and respect for the Travelers, and if so they will receive a small token which they can show to any Traveler to be accepted amongst them.  Any traveler must honor an agreement with another traveler or Friend of the Travelers.  Note this honor may be revoked if they betray the trust of the Travelers in any way.

**Goblins**
Although generally hostile to all other races, the goblin kingdom are primarily interested in the goblins.  They are mischievous and love to create mayhem any chance they can, and to steal gold.  Their main adversary are the Fomorians.  Like the elves and other fey, the Fomorians are their ancient enemy.  They are more than willing to ally with other elves against the Fomorians as long as it aligns with their interests.  However, there ambitions are usually short term, and they are often preoccupied with stealing from other races, kidnapping, and tormenting them any way they can.

**Fomorians**
Army of demons led by Lord Lokor, engaged in eternal war with the elves for thousands of years.  They and their agents attack and harass many human kingdoms directly, but their main power comes from a network of spies and agents influencing political events from behind the scenes.  Their objective is to push the human, elven and dwarven kingdoms into open war with eachother, allowing them to finish eachother off before attacking directly.

**Cult of Lokor**
A highly secretive cult working in the shadows to promote the interests of Lokor, and also through rituals and sacrifices summon demons to add to their army.

# Characters
## The Keep
Garrison – The keep has a garrison of 24 soldiers, 2 level 3, 4 level 2, and the rest are 1st level,  Here is a list of the notable soldiers:
Garth LocMalean – 3rd level fighter originally from Talerin.  2 other of his clansman from clan LocMalean are also with them, his younger cousin Arleath and nephew Kelor, both 2nd and 1st level respectively. Together they frequent the in and enjoy drinking and gambling a bit more than the other soldiers in the garrison.
Oliver Smith – 3rd level fighter from Arnsberg, who was orphaned as a child and found purpose and family in the Arnish army.  As a youth he was a pickpocket and part of the thieves’ guild.  He has since reformed and holds a particular resentment for the guild and scofflaws in general.  His parents we in fact well off but were killed in a burglary gone wrong, for which he holds most thieves responsible.  However, many of his friends were thieves growing up on the streets of Arnsburg and had no other choice but to steal to survive, so he still sees both sides, but the murder of his parents has shaped his personality deeply.

Zarnoch Hamasean – 2nd level fighter from the far south in the sea of sands.  Spent a long life at sea before being shipwrecked outside Arnsburg.  Having no other choice he took up a life in the army, using skills he learned at sea.  He is a pious man and diligent soldier, and he does not drink or gamble.

Gwenyth the dwarf – 2nd level dwarvish fighter from Undor.  Gwenyth was an adventurer who lost her party in a wandering monster encounter nearby in the mountains.  The party was surprised by a group of wandering trolls.  They were no match for the trolls and she barely survived.  The lone survivor, she managed to find the keep where she eventually joined the garrison as a soldier, tired of her days of adventuring.  However, she has a lot of good advice for the party if asked, mainly on how NOT to get the whole party killed in the wilderness.  Most of her advice is about being well prepared and provisioned.  It’s the boring stuff that will kill you.

Arleath LocMalean – 2nd level fighter from Talerin, Garth’s cousin.
