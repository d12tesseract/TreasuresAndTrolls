Editing help and format

Use d for a normal door, x for a secret door, c for a concealed door, and C for a chest. Paint two adjacent b cells for one door centered on their shared edge. A lone b and incomplete spiral stairs may be saved as ASCII drafts, but cannot be exported as SVG.

Doorways require opposite floor, trap, or chest squares and stone on their other sides. Diagonals need paired open or stone neighbors. Partitions require literal smooth floor on their left or bottom. Invalid ordinary geometry is rejected without changing the map.

Spirals use S (up), s (down), or Z (both), in 2 x 2, 3 x 3, or 4 x 4 footprints. Leave a literal-space corner landing (2 x 2 landing for a 4 x 4 spiral). A 3 x 3 center is space or stone. Paint the landing and center before completing the footprint.

Resize keeps the top-left corner fixed; expansion adds stone. Move contents drags the map's actual terrain by whole squares. If a move would lose any non-stone squares, expand the map first. Undo/redo also restores resized or moved maps. Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y.

Feet per square controls physical SVG scale, not screen zoom. ASCII stores terrain only; keep or reselect the scale after loading. SVG export uses 50 feet per inch. Maps support up to 16,384 cells, with each dimension at most 256.
