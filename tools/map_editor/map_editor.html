Editing help and format

Use d for a normal door, x for a secret door, c for a concealed door, and C for a chest. Paint a straight line of adjacent b cells for one centered door along that line. A lone b and incomplete spiral stairs may be saved as ASCII drafts, but cannot be exported as SVG.

Identical normal, secret, or concealed door tiles form one horizontal or vertical doorway. Door lines may be painted in stone or open terrain without surrounding-floor requirements. Diagonals need paired open or stone neighbors. Wall partitions may touch any terrain. Edge doors require literal smooth floor on their left or bottom. Invalid ordinary geometry is rejected without changing the map.

Straight stairs use E/e north, F/f east, G/g south, H/h west; ramps use O/o north, P/p east, Q/q south, R/r west. Uppercase goes up and lowercase goes down in that direction. Touching identical tiles form one stairway or ramp, with one square of elevation change per square along its direction. Downward stair treads narrow toward the arrowhead to half their starting width; upward stair treads stay equal in width. Irregular groups are clipped to their painted footprint.

Spirals use S (up), s (down), or Z (both), in 2 x 2, 3 x 3, or 4 x 4 footprints. Leave a literal-space corner landing (2 x 2 landing for a 4 x 4 spiral). A 3 x 3 center is space or stone. Paint the landing and center before completing the footprint.

Resize keeps the top-left corner fixed; expansion adds stone. Move contents drags the map's actual terrain and labels by whole squares. If a move would lose any non-stone squares or labels, expand the map first. Undo/redo also restores resized or moved maps. Shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y.

Right-click any square to add, edit, or remove its label, including on stone. Display labels controls the preview and SVG export, not ASCII saving. Labels follow one blank line after the terrain as x,y: text, with 0,0 at the top-left.

Brush sizes are 1 x 1, 2 x 2, 3 x 3, and 5 x 5. The 2 x 2 brush starts at the cursor square and extends right and down; larger brushes are centered on the cursor. Zoom changes only the view, from 25% to 400%, not map contents or SVG export scale.

Feet per square controls physical SVG scale, not screen zoom. ASCII stores terrain and labels; keep or reselect the scale after loading. SVG export uses 50 feet per inch. Maps support up to 512 x 512 squares (262,144 cells).

Cavern SVG walls use continuous curved random walks, sampled at quarter-square intervals with up to half a square of displacement on either side of the original boundary. Smooth wall joins remain grid-aligned. The editing preview keeps its simplified grid geometry.
